## About

I'm a software architect and the founder of **Colee Software Services LLC**, a digital infrastructure and software consultancy in McMinnville, Oregon. I design and build production web platforms — primarily on Next.js, TypeScript, and Node — with an emphasis on performance, accessibility, search visibility, and regulatory fit.

Every engagement starts with the same question: what does this system have to do for the business? From there the work is architecture — domain model and data contracts first, a performance budget second, interface last. I favor opinionated stacks, strong typing, and clear boundaries between services, and I deploy to infrastructure that I operate and monitor.

Outside client work, I'm completing a master's degree in computer science.

---

## Engineering Principles

- **Architecture first.** Model the domain, define the contracts, then choose the stack that fits the problem — never the reverse.
- **Performance is a requirement.** Core Web Vitals targets, edge caching, and a performance budget on every build.
- **Accessibility by default.** WCAG 2.2 AA is built into components and content from the first commit, not retrofitted.
- **Built for search and answer engines.** Semantic HTML, structured data, and content architecture designed for Google and AI answer engines alike.
- **Compliance as a design constraint.** HIPAA, TCPA, SEC/FINRA advertising rules, and ADA requirements are settled at the architecture stage, not in a final review.
- **Typed end to end.** TypeScript across the stack, schema validation at every boundary, and tests where failures would be expensive.
- **Automate the repeatable.** MCP servers, agents, and pipelines that turn recurring operational work into reliable, observable workflows.

## Standards and Compliance

Regulated engagements are scoped and built against the rules that govern them, beginning with the first design review.

| Standard | Applies to | In practice |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| **HIPAA** | Healthcare and health-adjacent platforms | No PHI through the web layer, privacy-respecting analytics, compliant contact and booking flows, and business associate agreement awareness |
| **TCPA** | Lead generation and SMS/email outreach pipelines | Explicit written consent, timestamped opt-in records, do-not-call scrubbing, and no autodialed or prerecorded contact without consent |
| **SEC Rule 206(4)-1 / FINRA** | Registered investment advisors | No performance claims or implied returns, Form ADV disclosure linking, testimonial-free copy, and advertising-rule-aware language throughout |
| **ADA / WCAG 2.2 AA** | Every build | Accessible markup, keyboard navigation, screen-reader compatibility, and contrast compliance by default |
| **Investigative standards** | Digital forensics and OSINT engagements | Documented methodology, chain of custody, defensible sourcing, and findings formatted for legal handoff where applicable |
