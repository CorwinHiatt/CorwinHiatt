## About

I'm a software architect and the founder and CEO of **Colee Software Services LLC**, a digital infrastructure and software consultancy in McMinnville, Oregon. Colee designs, builds, hosts, and monitors custom software and web platforms, and provides technical SEO and AI search visibility, accessibility and regulatory compliance, and threat intelligence through its KPG Safeguard division. The firm is insured for cyber and general liability and is Clutch verified.

My work starts with what a system has to do for the business: domain model and data contracts first, a performance budget second, interface last. I favor opinionated stacks, strong typing, clear boundaries between services, and infrastructure I operate and monitor myself. Outside client work, I'm completing a master's degree in computer science.

**Build · Reach · Defend** — [coleesoftwareservices.com](https://www.coleesoftwareservices.com/)

## What I Do

| Area | Scope |
|------|-------|

| **Technical SEO, AEO, and AI search visibility** | Structured data, crawl and index management, answer-engine optimization, AI-citation tracking, full digital-presence audits, and lead-generation funnels with consent-compliant outreach |
| **Accessibility and regulatory compliance** | WCAG 2.2 AA audits and remediation, and builds aligned with HIPAA, TCPA, and SEC/FINRA advertising rules |
| **Infrastructure and hosting operations** | DNS consolidation on Cloudflare, Vercel and managed hosting, transactional email and DMARC, monitoring, and monthly performance reporting |
| **AI integration and automation** | MCP servers, LLM-backed workflows, API orchestration, and agentic tooling that turn recurring operational work into reliable pipelines |
| **Intelligence and digital forensics** | OSINT, digital footprint analysis, real-time threat monitoring, and documented, defensible investigative workflows |
| **Technology leadership** | Fractional CTO and IT/compliance leadership, including Microsoft 365 tenant architecture and CMMC-aligned compliance for a prime government contractor |

Regulated work is built to the rules that govern it: HIPAA-aware data handling for healthcare, TCPA consent mechanics for outreach, SEC Rule 206(4)-1 and FINRA advertising rules for investment advisors, WCAG 2.2 AA on every build, and chain-of-custody methodology for investigative work.
